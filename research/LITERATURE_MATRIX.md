Include peer-reviewed Rust-specific evidence and operational documentation separately. Rules are local engineering policy informed by sources, not claims that every rule was experimentally demonstrated. Reassess applicability, publication version and contrary evidence before promotion.

## Search and update protocol
Record database, query, date, screening inclusion/exclusion, DOI, accessible version, method/sample, limitations, and candidate policy changes. Search ACM DL, IEEE Xplore, institutional repositories and Rust reference materials for safety, concurrency, testing, dependencies, performance and portability. Distinguish empirical observation, proof assumptions, tool documentation and local policy. Obtain full text where lawful and record page/section before claiming detailed provisions. Track negative findings and conflicting evidence; do not use popularity as quality evidence. Review on material toolchain changes or at least semiannually.
